Our paid courses are hosted on Udemy — open any course from the Courses page. For the current 30% discount, use the coupon links on the Offers page; the code is already applied.

Is the YouTube content free?

Yes. Our YouTube courses and playlists are free to watch. Channel members also get Silver and Gold members-only videos.

Do you offer consulting for companies?

Yes — consulting for businesses and individuals across OIC, VBCS, ORDS, OPA, Fusion and OCI, from troubleshooting to architecture reviews. Submit a service request or email us.

How do payments and refunds work?

Payments are handled by third-party platforms (YouTube, Udemy, PayPal, BuyMeACoffee, Ko-fi, UPI), and refunds follow the policies of those platforms. See our Terms & Conditions.

We're based in Mumbai, India and support learners worldwide online, Monday – Friday.
